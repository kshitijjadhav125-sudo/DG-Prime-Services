What we check

A background verification report is only useful if it's accurate. We confirm each result against the original source before it reaches you.

Employment history

We confirm job titles, dates, and reasons for leaving directly with past employers.

Criminal record

Checks run against available court and registry records for the candidate's jurisdictions.

Education & credentials

Degrees, certifications, and licenses are confirmed with the issuing institution.

Identity verification

Government ID and address details are cross-checked for consistency.

Reference checks

Structured calls with the references a candidate provides, summarized clearly.

Address history

Current and previous addresses are confirmed for tenancy and residency checks.

How a check moves

Three steps, start to finish. You'll know where things stand at every one of them.

  1. Submit the request

    Send us the candidate's details and the checks you need. Most requests take under five minutes to submit.

  2. We verify

    Our team contacts employers, institutions, and registries directly — no automated guesses.

  3. You get the report

    A clear, plain-language report lands in your inbox, flagging anything that needs a closer look.
