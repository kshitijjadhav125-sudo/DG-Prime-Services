About BGV

Verification you can put your name behind.

BGV was started to fix a simple problem: too many background checks are just database lookups dressed up as due diligence. We verify with the actual source, every time.

Why we exist

A bad hire, an unreliable tenant, or a falsified credential costs far more than the check that would have caught it. BGV exists so employers, landlords, and agencies can make that decision with confidence — backed by a report that was actually verified, not just matched against a database.

How we work

Every check is carried out by a person who contacts the original source — the employer, the school, the registry — directly. Reports are reviewed before they're sent, so what lands in your inbox is something you can act on immediately.

What we stand for

Accuracy over speed

A fast wrong answer is worse than a slightly slower right one. We verify, not just search.

Plain-language reports

No jargon or unexplained codes — every report tells you clearly what was found and what it means.

Respect for candidates

Verification is done fairly, with consent, and within the law wherever we operate.

Reach out — we're happy to walk through our process before you commit to anything.
