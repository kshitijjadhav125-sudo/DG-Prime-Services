Services

Verification services for every stage of a decision.

Order one check or a full package. Every report is verified against the original source, not just a database match.

Employment verification

We contact former employers directly to confirm job titles, employment dates, salary (where permitted), and eligibility for rehire. Useful for any role where a candidate's work history needs to hold up.

Criminal record check

Searches available court and registry records across the jurisdictions a candidate has lived or worked in. Results are reviewed by a person before they're sent to you.

Education & credential verification

Degrees, diplomas, certifications, and professional licenses are confirmed directly with the issuing school, board, or authority.

Identity verification

Government-issued ID is checked for authenticity and cross-referenced against the details a candidate has provided.

Reference checks

We call the professional or personal references a candidate lists and provide a written summary of each conversation.

Address & residency history

Confirms current and past addresses — useful for tenant screening and residency-dependent roles.
